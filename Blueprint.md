# Blueprint: local class field theory for finite extensions of ℚ_p

Status keys: **done** (proved, no `sorry`), **wip**, **planned**.
Lean names are given once a milestone is proved.

## 0. Survey of Mathlib v4.32.0 (commit 81a5d25) and choice of route

What Mathlib has that we use:

* `spectralNorm ℚ_[p] K` (`Mathlib/Analysis/Normed/Unbundled/SpectralNorm.lean`): the unique
  power-multiplicative extension of `‖·‖_p` to an algebraic extension, multiplicative
  (`spectralMulAlgNorm`), nonarchimedean, depends only on the minimal polynomial over `ℚ_[p]`
  (hence is automatically compatible with towers and invariant under automorphisms), and
  `spectralNorm_eq_norm_coeff_zero_rpow` (`|x| = |a₀|^{1/deg}`).
* Dedekind domains, `Ideal.ramificationIdx`, `Ideal.inertiaDeg`, `sum_ramification_inertia`,
  integral closures (`integralClosure ℤ_[p] K` is a Dedekind domain, finite over `ℤ_[p]`).
* Galois theory incl. infinite Galois theory with the Krull topology
  (`Mathlib/FieldTheory/Galois/Infinite.lean`), finite fields (Galois group cyclic, generated by
  Frobenius), `IsArithFrobAt`, `Ideal.inertia`.
* Group cohomology in low degree, Hilbert 90 (`groupCohomology.exists_div_of_norm_eq_one`
  for cyclic Galois groups), Tate cohomology (definition only), periodicity for cyclic groups.
* `IsNonarchimedeanLocalField` (`Mathlib/NumberTheory/LocalField/Basic.lean`): only the
  definition and a handful of consequences for a *single* field; **no** finite extensions,
  unramified extensions, Frobenius for local fields, Herbrand quotients, Brauer groups of local
  fields, Lubin–Tate formal groups (only `FormalGroup` the structure), or any class field theory.

Route. Every finite extension of a finite extension of `ℚ_p` is again finite over `ℚ_p`, so we work
in the concrete setting `[Field K] [Algebra ℚ_[p] K] [FiniteDimensional ℚ_[p] K]`, with *all*
structure derived from the `ℚ_[p]`-algebra structure: absolute value `‖x‖_K = spectralNorm ℚ_[p] K x`,
ring of integers `𝒪 K = integralClosure ℤ_[p] K`. This avoids constructing valuative/topological
instances on extensions (no instance diamonds), and lets us use Mathlib's Dedekind-domain
ramification theory.

For reciprocity we follow the "class field axiom" route (Neukirch, ANT IV §§4–6, specialised to
local fields and to finite unramified levels instead of `K^ur`), because it needs only the
*cyclic* Tate groups `Ĥ⁰`, `Ĥ⁻¹`, which we treat concretely (a commutative group with an
automorphism of finite order); no `H²`, cup products, Brauer groups, or formal groups are
needed. Herbrand quotients give the class field axiom `[Kˣ : N Lˣ] = [L : K]` for cyclic `L/K`
(Serre, Local Fields, VIII & XIII §2; Neukirch V.1), from which unramified norm surjectivity on
units also follows. The existence theorem (Neukirch V.1 via Kummer theory, or Lubin–Tate) is
the last stage.

## A. Local fields finite over ℚ_p (foundations)

* A1 **done** (`PadicField/Norm.lean`: `PadicField.nrm`, `nrm_add_le_max`, `nrm_map`,
  `exists_nrm_pow_natDegree_eq`) `padicAbs` on an algebraic extension of `ℚ_[p]`: multiplicative, nonarchimedean,
  extends `‖·‖_p`, invariant under `ℚ_[p]`-algebra maps (towers, Galois action).
* A2 **done** (`Integers.lean`, `Uniformizer.lean`, `Ramification.lean`: `PadicField.Integers` =
  `𝒪 p K` as the closed unit ball (a `ValuationSubring`), `Integers.isDiscreteValuationRing`,
  `finite_residue`, `q`). Originally planned as `𝒪 K = integralClosure ℤ_[p] K`; `x ∈ 𝒪 K ↔ ‖x‖ ≤ 1`; `𝒪 K` local, DVR, finite
  residue field `𝓴 K` of cardinality `q_K`; value group discrete.
* A3 **done** except the unit filtration (`Uniformizer.lean`: `PadicField.v`, `v_mul`,
  `IsUniformizer`, `exists_uniformizer`, `eq_zpow_mul_unit`, `mem_maximalIdeal_pow_iff`)
  normalised valuation `v_K : Kˣ →* Multiplicative ℤ` (or `→+ ℤ` on `Additive`),
  uniformisers, `𝒪ˣ = ker v`, unit filtration `U^(n)`.
* A4 **done** (`Complete.lean`: `exists_limit`, `Integers.isAdicComplete`,
  `Integers.henselianLocalRing`; `Teichmuller.lean`: `Teichmuller.teichmuller`) completeness of `K` (finite-dimensional over complete `ℚ_[p]`), Hensel's lemma for
  `𝒪 K` (simple roots), Teichmüller roots of unity `μ_{q-1} ⊆ K`.
* A5 **done** (`Extension.lean`, `Ramification.lean`, `FieldNorm.lean`, `Tower.lean`:
  `Integers.isIntegralClosure`, `Integers.moduleFinite`, `e_mul_f`, `v_algebraMap`, `nrm_norm`,
  `v_norm`, `nrm_algEquiv_tower`, `e_mul_e`, `f_mul_f`) extensions `L/K`: `e(L/K)`, `f(L/K)`, `e f = [L:K]`, `v_K (N_{L/K} x) = f · v_L x`,
  Galois action preserves `𝒪 L`, `v_L`.

## B. Unramified extensions, Frobenius, inertia

* B1 **wip**: done: unramified ⇒ splitting field of `X^Q - X`, Galois
  (`Unramified.lean`: `isSplittingField_of_isUnramified`, `isGalois_of_isUnramified`);
  `residueGal` surjective with kernel `inertia` for any Galois `L/K`, bijective if unramified
  (`Galois.lean`). Remaining: existence of an unramified extension of each degree.
  `L/K` unramified (`e = 1`) ⇔ `L = K(ζ)` with `ζ` a primitive `(q^f - 1)`-st root of
  unity; unramified ⇒ Galois with `Gal(L/K) ≅ Gal(𝓴 L/𝓴 K)` cyclic.
* B2 **done** (`Galois.lean`: `frob`, `frob_spec`, `eq_frob_iff`, `orderOf_frob`, `zpowers_frob`,
  `isCyclic_gal`) arithmetic Frobenius `Frob_{L/K}`: unique `σ` with `σ x ≡ x^q mod 𝔪_L` on `𝒪 L`.
* B3 **planned** existence and uniqueness of the unramified extension of each degree inside a fixed
  algebraic closure `Ω`; `K^ur ⊆ Ω`; `Gal(K^ur/K)` procyclic topologically generated by Frobenius
  (`≅ ℤ̂`).
* B4 **planned** inertia subgroup `I_K ≤ Gal(Ω/K)` (valuation-theoretic) equals `Gal(Ω/K^ur)`;
  unramified quotient `Gal(Ω/K) ⧸ I_K ≃ Gal(K^ur/K)`.

## C. Herbrand quotients and the class field axiom

* C1 **done** (`Herbrand/Basic.lean`: `Herbrand.H0`, `Herbrand.Hm1`, `herbrand`) concrete cyclic Tate groups for `(M, σ)` with `σ^n = 1`: `Ĥ⁰ = ker(σ-1)/im N`,
  `Ĥ⁻¹ = ker N / im(σ-1)`; Herbrand quotient `h`.
* C2 **done** (`Herbrand/Exact.lean`, `Herbrand/Examples.lean`: `herbrand_eq_mul_of_ses`,
  `herbrand_eq_one_of_finite`, `herbrand_int`, `herbrand_induced`,
  `herbrand_eq_of_finite_quotient`) `h` multiplicative in short exact sequences; `h(finite) = 1`; `h(ℤ, trivial) = n`.
* C3 **planned** successive approximation: an open, complete filtered `G`-submodule of `𝒪ˣ_L` with
  induced graded pieces has trivial `Ĥ⁰`, `Ĥ⁻¹` (normal basis + filtration argument).
* C4 **planned** `h(𝒪ˣ_L) = 1`, `h(Lˣ) = [L:K]` for cyclic `L/K`; Hilbert 90 ⇒
  `[Kˣ : N_{L/K} Lˣ] = [L:K]` (class field axiom).
* C5 **planned** consequences: unramified `L/K` ⇒ `N_{L/K} Lˣ = ⟨π^n⟩ × 𝒪ˣ_K`, in particular
  `N(𝒪ˣ_L) = 𝒪ˣ_K`; second inequality `[Kˣ : N Lˣ] ≤ [L:K]` for abelian (solvable) `L/K`.

## D. Reciprocity for finite abelian extensions

* D1 **planned** unramified reciprocity: `Kˣ/N Lˣ ≃ Gal(L/K)`, `x ↦ Frob^{v(x)}`.
* D2 **planned** Neukirch map `r_{L/K} : Gal(L/K) → Kˣ/N Lˣ`, `σ ↦ N_{Σ/K}(π_Σ)` where `Σ` is the
  fixed field of a Frobenius lift of `σ` in `L K_m` (`m` large); well defined.
* D3 **planned** `r_{L/K}` is a homomorphism and, for abelian `L/K`, an isomorphism.
* D4 **planned** functoriality: restriction for `L ⊆ L'`, norms for `K'/K`.

## E. The Artin map and its characterisation

* E1 **planned** `K^ab ⊆ Ω`; `artin K : Kˣ →* Gal(K^ab/K)`, continuous (preimages of open subgroups
  contain some `U^(n)`), dense image.
* E2 **planned** uniformisers ↦ arithmetic Frobenius mod inertia; `artin(𝒪ˣ_K) = Gal(K^ab/K^ur)`.
* E3 **planned** norm compatibility `artin_{K'}(x)|_{K^ab} = artin_K(N_{K'/K} x)`.

## F. Existence theorem

* F1 **planned** every open finite-index subgroup of `Kˣ` is a norm group; `artin` is injective,
  `𝒪ˣ_K ≃ inertia of Gal(K^ab/K)`.
